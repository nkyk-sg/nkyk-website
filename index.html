Founder

NAKASHIMA YUKI

Founder & Director

With a career spanning B2B technology sales, consumer internet, and fintech, he brings a breadth of experience to every endeavor. Starting at Keyence Corporation, he built a strong foundation in B2B sales of image processing sensors and earned multiple sales excellence awards, before moving to LINE Corporation, where he led the launch of financial services and spearheaded key marketing initiatives.

He subsequently served as Head of New Business Development at Coincheck, Japan's largest cryptocurrency exchange, contributing to revenue growth of several billion yen. In July 2024, he relocated to Singapore and founded NKYK PTE. LTD.
